02 / Motion language

Movement with meaning.

Ideas in Motion is where MINI LAB explores how digital experiences move and respond. We experiment with animation, visual systems, transitions and the small moments between one screen and the next.

Our direction is restrained: movement should guide attention, explain a change or give an interface a little character. It should never make a simple task harder.

Experiments may include logo studies, animated symbols, interface prototypes, loading states and short motion pieces. This section will grow as we create and document new work.

01 / 01 / RHYTHM

Purposeful timing

Transitions should feel considered and give people enough time to understand what changed.

02 / 02 / FORM

A consistent visual world

Geometric shapes, strong typography and a focused palette help each experiment feel connected.

03 / 03 / FEEDBACK

Motion that responds

Hover, tap and scroll interactions should make the interface feel alive and understandable.

04 / 04 / RESTRAINT

Less, but better

We avoid animation for animation's sake and respect reduced-motion preferences.

A MINI LAB principle

Motion is not decoration alone. At its best, it makes an interaction easier to understand.
