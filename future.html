03 / The roadmap

The next idea starts here.

Room to Explore is our open space for future ideas. Not every experiment becomes a product, and that's part of the process. We want to test concepts, learn from them and build the ones that genuinely deserve to exist.

Our roadmap is intentionally flexible. We will share progress honestly, publish projects when they're ready and keep this page updated as the lab evolves.

Have an idea or want to follow the work? Find MINI LAB on Instagram. We welcome thoughtful feedback and creative conversations.
