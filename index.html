the principles

LESS, BUT CONSIDERED.

these are the promises we're building toward. we'll keep them honest as the studio grows.

FREE TO USE

Core tools stay free. No subscription walls for basic features.

NO ADS

No banners or attention-grabbing promotions inside our apps.

PRIVACY-MINDED

Prefer on-device storage and collect as little data as practical.

MADE WITH CARE

Fast, readable interfaces where every detail has a reason.
